Problem & Value For management

Why e-Sign

The current paper sign-and-route process is slow, hard to trace, and carries compliance risk. e-Sign addresses all three: speed, control, and legality.

🐢 Current problems

  • Paper routing across many levels is slow and easily lost.
  • Hard to trace who approved, who signed, and when.
  • Legal risk when documents are stored or signed improperly.

⚡ What e-Sign does

  • Digital multi-level approval, routed by amount threshold.
  • Digital / electronic signing by title, with integrity sealing.
  • Automatic retention on schedule, instant lookup.

✓ Value delivered

  • Shorter approval time, work anywhere, anytime.
  • Non-repudiation & integrity — audit/inspection ready.
  • Legal value equivalent to paper documents.
Legal basis For management

Compliance foundation under Vietnamese law

The model is designed to follow five core legal instruments. This is what makes the product distinctive and safe.

Law 20/2023/QH15 · effective 01/07/2024

Law on E-Transactions 2023

An e-signature is not denied legal value merely for being electronic. Three types: specialized e-signature, public digital signature, public-duty specialized digital signature.

Decree 23/2025/ND-CP · effective 10/04/2025

E-signatures & trust services

Governs trust services: timestamping, data-message certification, and public digital signature certification.

Law 88/2015/QH13 · Articles 17, 19, 41

Accounting Law 2015

Electronic documents are lawful when bearing an e-signature; complete signatures by title; safe retention, retrievable and printable when needed.

Decree 174/2016/ND-CP

Document retention periods

Retention classes: minimum 5 years, minimum 10 years, or permanent — depending on the accounting document type.

Decree 123/2020/ND-CP

Electronic invoices & documents

Applies when integrating e-invoices: digital signatures on invoices, format, signing time, retention, and lookup.

Special situation

When the signer is absent

Non-negotiable principle: never lend the USB Token / private key for someone to sign on your behalf. Handle by degree of absence.

Situation A Away but reachable

Remote signing (Cloud CA / SmartCA) via phone + OTP/biometrics. Still the owner signing — no delegation, no added risk. Prefer this path.

Situation B Unable to sign (long leave/illness)

Delegate to a backup who signs with their own certificate, with a delegation letter stating scope + amount limit + validity, marked "on behalf / p.p.", with a full audit trail.

Legal note: the legal representative's signature (e.g., signing financial statements) cannot be freely delegated — it must follow the company charter & the Enterprise Law. Lock/restrict delegation for sensitive document groups.
